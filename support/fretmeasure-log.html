What FretMeasure Log Does

FretMeasure Log records guitar and bass setup measurements, string history, service notes, and copyable setup cards.

Important Note

FretMeasure Log records your own measurements and notes. It does not provide repair instructions, professional advice, safety guidance, or recommended adjustment values.

Privacy

No account, ads, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs are used.
