What BoltPlan Does

BoltPlan estimates fabric yardage from rectangular pattern-piece dimensions, bolt width, seam allowance, quantities, and purchase increments.

Important Note

BoltPlan is an estimating aid. Always verify pattern instructions, grainline, nap, print direction, shrinkage, and irregular pattern shapes before cutting fabric.

Privacy

No account, ads, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs are used.
