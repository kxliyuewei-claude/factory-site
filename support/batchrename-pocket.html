FAQ

Does the app modify my original files?
No. It exports renamed copies and leaves originals untouched.

Does it rename photos inside Apple Photos?
No. It works with files selected through the Files app and does not modify Apple Photos internal filenames.

Does it upload my files?
No. BatchRename Pocket is local-first and does not upload files.

Is there an account or subscription?
No. The app is a paid download with no account, no subscription, and no in-app purchases.
