WallMark Support

WallMark helps you calculate nail marks for frames, art rows, simple gallery wall grids, two-hook frames, and wire drops before you make holes.

Common Questions

Do I need an account?
No. WallMark works locally on your device.

Does WallMark use subscriptions or in-app purchases?
No. WallMark is sold as a paid-upfront App Store app.

Does WallMark sync layouts to the cloud?
No. Saved layouts are stored locally on your device.

Does WallMark detect studs or recommend anchors?
No. WallMark is a layout calculator only. Always choose safe hardware and installation methods for your wall.

Which measurements can I use?
You can work in inches or centimeters.
