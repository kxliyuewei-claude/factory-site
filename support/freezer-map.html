Freezer Map Support

Freezer Map helps you record what is in your chest or garage freezer and where it is stored.

Common Questions

Do I need an account?
No. Freezer Map works locally on your device.

Does Freezer Map sync with other people?
No. The first version is a simple local tool with no cloud sync.

Does it decide whether food is safe to eat?
No. Freezer Map only records what you enter. It does not provide food safety, health, nutrition, or expiration advice.

Does it have subscriptions or in-app purchases?
No. Freezer Map is sold as a paid-upfront App Store app.

Support

If you need support, contact the developer through the App Store listing or include Freezer Map in your message.
