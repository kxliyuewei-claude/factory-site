BoltPlan Yardage Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-09

BoltPlan Yardage is a local-first sewing utility. It does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or track personal data.

Data Collection

BoltPlan Yardage does not collect data from users. Fabric plans are stored locally on your device and are not sent to the developer, a server, or a third party.

Tracking

BoltPlan Yardage does not track users across apps or websites.

Third-Party SDKs

BoltPlan Yardage does not use third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.

Accounts

BoltPlan Yardage does not require account creation or login.
