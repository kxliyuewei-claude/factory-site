WallMark Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 9, 2026

WallMark is a local-first iOS app for calculating picture hanging layouts and nail mark positions.

Data Collection

WallMark does not collect personal data.

The app does not use accounts, analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, third-party SDKs, cloud sync, or external servers.

Local Data

If you save a layout, the layout information you enter is stored locally on your device. This may include measurements, layout names, frame sizes, spacing, and hanger settings. This data is not sent to us.

Tracking

WallMark does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

WallMark is not directed to children and does not collect data from children.

System Permissions

WallMark does not request access to location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone, Bluetooth, health data, calendar, reminders, or notifications.

Installation Safety

WallMark provides layout measurements only. It does not detect studs, judge wall material, select anchors, or guarantee installation safety. You are responsible for choosing appropriate hardware and installation methods for your wall.
