KilnCost Log Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-09

KilnCost Log is a local-first pottery studio utility. It does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or track personal data.

Data Collection

KilnCost Log does not collect data from users. Kiln profiles, firing estimates, load items, and firing logs are stored locally on your device and are not sent to the developer, a server, or a third party.

Tracking

KilnCost Log does not track users across apps or websites.

Third-Party SDKs

KilnCost Log does not use third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.

Accounts

KilnCost Log does not require account creation or login.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact kxliyuewei@gmail.com.
