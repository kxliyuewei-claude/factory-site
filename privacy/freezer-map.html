Freezer Map Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 8, 2026

Freezer Map is a local-first iOS app for recording freezer items and their locations.

Data Collection

Freezer Map does not collect personal data.

The freezer items, notes, quantities, zones, and frozen dates you enter are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to a server by Freezer Map.

Tracking

Freezer Map does not track you across apps or websites.

Accounts

Freezer Map does not use accounts or sign-in.

Analytics and Advertising

Freezer Map does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs.

System Permissions

Freezer Map does not request access to location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone, Bluetooth, health data, calendar, reminders, or notifications.

Food Safety

Freezer Map is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide food safety, health, nutrition, or expiration advice.

Contact

For support, visit Freezer Map Support.
